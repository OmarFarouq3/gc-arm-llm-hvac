# Variable glossary

Used to explain variable codes in a rule (one line per code; unit number suffix _1.._3 is dropped).
This file is not part of the retrieval index; see src/rag.py.

- CHL_POW: chiller electrical power (kW)
- CHL_SW_TEMP: chilled water leaving (supply) temperature of a chiller (degF)
- CHL_RW_TEMP: chilled water entering (return) temperature of a chiller (degF)
- CHL_CW_FLOW: chilled water flow through a chiller (GPM)
- CHL_SWCD_TEMP: condenser water leaving a chiller (degF)
- CHL_RWCD_TEMP: condenser water entering a chiller (degF)
- CHL_CD_FLOW: condenser water flow through a chiller (GPM)
- CHL_COMP_SPD_CTRL: chiller compressor speed control signal (0-1)
- CHL_STA: chiller on/off status (0/1)
- CT_POW: cooling tower fan power (kW)
- CT_FAN_SPD: cooling tower fan speed (0-1)
- CT_FAN_SPD_CTRL: cooling tower fan speed control signal (0-1)
- CT_FLOW: water flow through a cooling tower (GPM)
- CT_SW_TEMP: water leaving (supply) temperature of a cooling tower (degF)
- CT_RW_TEMP: water entering (return) temperature of a cooling tower (degF)
- CT_STA: cooling tower on/off status (0/1)
- CT_SW_TEMPSPT: cooling tower leaving water temperature set-point (degF)
- CDWL_CW_FLOW: condenser water loop flow (GPM)
- CDWL_SW_TEMP: water leaving the condenser water loop (degF)
- CDWL_RW_TEMP: water entering the condenser water loop (degF)
- CDWL_PM_POW: condenser water pump power (kW)
- CWL_PRI_CW_FLOW: primary chilled water loop flow (GPM)
- CWL_PRI_SW_TEMP: primary loop supply water temperature (degF)
- CWL_PRI_RW_TEMP: primary loop return water temperature (degF)
- CWL_PRI_PM_POW: primary chilled water pump power (kW)
- CWL_PRI_SW_TEMPSPT: chilled water supply temperature set-point (degF)
- CWL_SEC_CW_FLOW: secondary chilled water loop flow (GPM)
- CWL_SEC_SW_TEMP: secondary loop supply water temperature (degF)
- CWL_SEC_RW_TEMP: secondary loop return water temperature (degF)
- CWL_SEC_DP: secondary loop differential pressure (inH2O)
- CWL_SEC_DPSPT: secondary loop differential pressure set-point (inH2O)
- CWL_SEC_LOAD: building cooling load from the secondary loop (W)
- CWL_SEC_PM_POW: secondary chilled water pump power (kW)
- CWL_SEC_PM_SPD: secondary chilled water pump speed (0-1)
- CWL_SEC_PM_STA: secondary pump on/off status (0/1)
- TWV_CTRL: condenser loop three-way bypass valve control signal (0-1)
- OA_TEMP: outdoor air dry-bulb temperature (degF)
- OA_TEMP_WB: outdoor air wet-bulb temperature (degF)
